How to play

  • Steer with AD / arrow keys, or the on-screen arrows.
  • Boost with Space / Shift. Hold it to pull away from the chaser.
  • Jump with W / ↑ after grabbing a green spring.
  • Honk with H or the HORN button; play the special dialogue with F or SPECIAL.
  • Bikes and carts: you can smash them (slows you down). Buses, trucks, cars with a red glow end the run.
  • Cars and light vehicles can be jumped. Buses and trucks cannot.
  • The red rickshaw behind you rams you when it catches up: big slowdown. Caught twice and it is over.
  • Pause with P / Esc.
